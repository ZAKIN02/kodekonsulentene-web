# Calibration Examples

Use these to show users how the framework maps to familiar stories and brands, and to calibrate the quality of drafted BrandScripts.

## Story Examples

**Star Wars (A New Hope / Empire):**
- Character: Luke. Wants to defeat the Empire.
- Problem: External: must defeat the Empire. Internal: is he really a Jedi? Philosophical: good vs. evil.
- Guide: Yoda (lived as a Jedi; gives confidence and training).
- Plan: trust the Force.
- Call to action: go defeat the Empire.
- Failure: the Rebellion is crushed. Success: the Rebellion avoids defeat; Death Star destroyed.

**Hunger Games:**
- Character: Katniss. Wants to survive the games.
- Problem: External: must survive. Internal: must stay good and authentic. Philosophical: tyranny vs. democracy.
- Guide: Haymitch (won the games before; gives confidence and a plan).
- Plan: endear the public to win sponsors.
- Call to action: compete.
- Failure: Katniss dies, District 12 crushed. Success: Katniss lives, District 12 rejoices.

Note the pattern: the guide has been where the hero is (empathy) and has won (competency).

## Brand Examples

**CarMax:**
- Character: a used-car buyer. Villain: the sleazy used car dealer experience.
- External: needs a reliable used car. Internal: fears being lied to and ripped off. Philosophical: honest people deserve honest deals.
- Guide: CarMax (empathy: no-haggle promise acknowledges the fear; competency: certified inspections, scale).
- Plan: agreement plan (no haggling, no pressure) + simple website to browse.
- CTA: buy a car. Failure: risk of the shady dealer. Success: a great car-buying experience.

**Allstate:**
- Character: anyone with property. Villain: Mayhem (chaos personified, brilliantly literal).
- Guide: Allstate. Plan: the Allstate value plan. CTA: get a quote.
- Failure: Mayhem might get you. Success: a happy life with no worries.

## Complete Worked Business Example

A boutique bookkeeping firm for restaurants:

**Controlling Idea:** Restaurant owners shouldn't lose their nights to spreadsheets; clean books should be as automatic as the dinner rush.

**One-Liner:** Most restaurant owners spend their only day off untangling the books and still don't know if they made money. We run done-for-you restaurant bookkeeping with a weekly profit snapshot, so owners always know their numbers and get their Sundays back.

1. **Character:** independent restaurant owners. Want: to know if they're profitable without doing the books. Aspirational identity: a real businessperson, not just a cook who owns a place.
2. **Problem:** Villain: the shoebox of receipts (financial chaos). External: books are months behind. Internal: feels embarrassed and anxious, suspects the business is leaking money. Philosophical: people who feed their community shouldn't be punished with paperwork.
3. **Guide:** Empathy: "You opened a restaurant to cook, not to do data entry at midnight." Competency: 120 restaurant clients, average 4% food-cost savings found in the first 90 days, QuickBooks ProAdvisor team.
4. **Plan ("The Clean Books Plan"):** 1. Book a 20-minute menu call. 2. We connect your POS and clean up the backlog. 3. Get your weekly profit snapshot every Monday.
5. **CTA:** Direct: "Book My Menu Call." Transitional: free PDF, "The 5 Numbers Every Restaurant Owner Must Know Weekly."
6. **Failure:** keep flying blind on food costs; tax-season scramble and penalties; another year of Sundays lost to spreadsheets.
7. **Success:** know your profit every Monday morning; catch food-cost leaks before they compound; walk into tax season already done. Transformation: From "anxious cook drowning in receipts" To "confident owner who knows the numbers."

Sound bites: "Get your Sundays back." "Know your profit every Monday." "You opened a restaurant to cook, not to do data entry."

Why this passes the quality bar: one desire, a personified villain, a feeling-level internal problem, empathy before competency, customer-action steps, an imperative CTA, a pinch of stakes, visual success, and a clean transformation. Every sound bite reinforces the controlling idea.
