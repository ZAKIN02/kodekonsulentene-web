Product photograph of one handmade miniature street block, matte clay and painted wood, about 60 cm wide, on a dark workbench, high three-quarter angle, 50mm lens, tack sharp. Left to right: a cobblestone street with tram rails and a small blue tram; three narrow townhouses with lit amber windows; a red-brick corner bakery with a striped awning and a small office with one large lit window above; behind the bakery a cobbled backyard with a cream wooden house and a white van with an open side door. Tiny clay figures with simple smooth faces, seen from behind or in profile: the courier in grey clothes with a mustard-yellow cap wheeling his bicycle on foot, a small yellow paper slip in his hand, no yellow except his cap and the paper slip; the customer outside the bakery, a woman with her dark hair in a bun, a brown coat and a beige tote bag; a plumber in blue overalls and a blue cap by the van. Deep blue dusk, warm amber glow. All surfaces blank and unmarked.
