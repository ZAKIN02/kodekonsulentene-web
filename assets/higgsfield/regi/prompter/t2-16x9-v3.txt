One continuous Steadicam shot. 0-4s: crane down from rooftop height to the cobblestones while the blue tram slides past close to the lens and wipes the frame. 4-10s: tracking shot at hip height along the lit shopfronts, following from behind the courier in the yellow cap as he wheels his bicycle, a yellow paper slip in his hand; figures cross the foreground. 10-13s: he leans the bicycle against the bakery wall and walks out of frame toward the door; the camera drifts to the customer who stands outside looking down at a phone. 13-15s: the camera stops and holds still on the end frame for the last two seconds. Figures are seen from behind or in profile, never facing the camera. Clay miniature, macro lens, steady amber light on blue dusk, smooth continuous motion, blank unmarked surfaces, screens show abstract colour shapes.
