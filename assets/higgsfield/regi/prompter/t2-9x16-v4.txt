One continuous vertical Steadicam shot along one street. 0-4s: crane down from high above the street to the cobblestones while the blue tram glides toward the lens and passes close by on the left. 4-10s: forward tracking shot at hip height straight down the street, following from behind the courier in the yellow cap as he wheels his bicycle, a yellow paper slip in his hand. 10-13s: he leans the bicycle against the bakery wall and walks out of frame; the camera drifts on to the same woman with the hair bun, brown coat and tote bag, who stands outside the bakery looking down at a phone. 13-15s: the camera stops and holds completely still on the end frame for the last two seconds. Figures are seen from behind or in profile, never facing the camera. Clay miniature, macro lens, steady amber light on blue dusk, smooth continuous motion, everything stays solid and opaque, blank unmarked surfaces.
