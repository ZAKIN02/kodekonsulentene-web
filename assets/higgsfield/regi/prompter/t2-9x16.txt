One continuous vertical Steadicam shot. 0-4s: crane down from rooftop height to the cobblestones while the blue tram glides toward the lens and passes close by on the left. 4-10s: forward tracking shot at hip height straight down the street, following from behind the bicycle courier in the yellow cap as he pushes his bike, a yellow paper slip in his hand; amber windows rise on both sides, figures cross the foreground. 10-15s: he parks the bike at the corner bakery and steps inside; the camera stays outside and slowly pushes in behind the customer who stands looking down at a phone, exactly on the end frame. Clay miniature, macro lens, shallow focus, steady amber light on blue dusk, smooth continuous motion, blank unmarked surfaces, screens show abstract colour shapes.
