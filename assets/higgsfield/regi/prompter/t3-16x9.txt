One continuous Steadicam shot. 0-5s: slow push in over the customer's shoulder to the phone, its screen a dull pale glow; she taps it, waits, gives up, lowers it and turns to walk away. 5-9s: the phone slowly brightens into warm colour shapes; she turns back and opens the bakery door, the camera follows her in at hip height. 9-15s: dolly sideways along the counter, loaves passing close to the lens; the baker hands her a paper bag in slow motion, then gives a yellow paper slip to the waiting courier. 15-20s: the camera glides on behind the counter and slowly pushes in to the small counter screen glowing lime green, exactly on the end frame. Clay miniature, macro lens, shallow focus, steady amber light on blue dusk, smooth continuous motion, blank unmarked surfaces, screens show abstract colour shapes.
