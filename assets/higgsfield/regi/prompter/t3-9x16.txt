One continuous vertical Steadicam shot. 0-5s: slow push in from behind the customer to the phone held up in the centre, its screen a dull pale glow; she taps it, waits, gives up, lowers it and turns to walk away. 5-9s: the phone slowly brightens into warm colour shapes; she turns back and opens the door straight ahead, the camera follows her in at hip height. 9-15s: push forward to the counter; the baker hands a paper bag down to her in slow motion, then gives a yellow paper slip to the waiting courier. 15-20s: the camera rises over the counter and slowly pushes in to the small counter screen glowing lime green, exactly on the end frame. Clay miniature, macro lens, shallow focus, steady amber light on blue dusk, smooth continuous motion, blank unmarked surfaces, screens show abstract colour shapes.
