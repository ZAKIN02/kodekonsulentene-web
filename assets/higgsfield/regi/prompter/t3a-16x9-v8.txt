The first frame is the start frame itself: same framing, same scale, same positions. One continuous shot from the same camera position, staying outside on the street. The courier at the left stands still beside his bicycle the whole shot, exactly where he is in the start frame. 0-1s: the camera is locked off; nothing moves. 1-3s: very slow, slight push in toward the woman; she taps her phone and waits, nothing happens on its screen. 3-4.5s: she lowers the phone to her side, her shoulders drop, and she turns her head away toward the street. 4.5-6.5s: the phone screen slowly changes to warm colours, the light stays inside the screen; she lifts the phone and looks at it. 6.5-8s: she walks to the bakery door at the right and closes her hand around the round brass door handle, the handle clearly visible; the camera pans gently with her. 8-10s: nobody moves; the camera stops and holds completely still for the last two seconds. Figures are seen from behind or in profile, never facing the camera. Clay miniature, steady amber light, smooth continuous motion, everything stays solid and opaque, blank unmarked surfaces.
