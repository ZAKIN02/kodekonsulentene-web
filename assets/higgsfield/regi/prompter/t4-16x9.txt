One continuous crane shot. 0-4s: crane straight up from the lime-green screen through the thin wooden ceiling into the office above; the courier lays a yellow paper slip on a clay woman's desk and leaves. 4-9s: rise and tilt to a top-down view directly over the desk: she types from the slip into a laptop, slides the slip to a tablet and types the same again, each screen a different colour. 9-15s: slow descending arc to over her shoulder; the screens merge into one calendar of colour blocks, a new block drops in, she puts the slip away and leans back. 15-20s: pan right to the window, dolly out over the sill and tilt down to the backyard, exactly on the end frame. Clay miniature, macro lens, shallow focus, steady amber light on blue dusk, smooth continuous motion, blank unmarked surfaces, screens show abstract colour shapes.
