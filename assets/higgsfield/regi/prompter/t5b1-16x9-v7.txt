The first frame is the start frame itself, same framing, same scale. One continuous shot at the open back of the white van, in one backyard at dusk; the van stays the same van with the same rear door. 0-3.5s: slow pull back from the gap in the row of yellow slips; the plumber in the blue cap, grey sweater and blue overalls has stood in front of the wall all along and comes into view from behind as the camera pulls back past his shoulder; he lowers his open hand and lifts a phone whose screen glows lime green with abstract bars; his shoulders drop. 3.5-6s: he puts the phone in his pocket and swings the rear door of the van shut in slow motion. 6-8s: nobody moves; the camera holds completely still on the closed door for the last two seconds. Figures are seen from behind or in profile, never facing the camera. Clay miniature, steady amber light, smooth continuous motion, everything stays solid and opaque, blank unmarked surfaces.
