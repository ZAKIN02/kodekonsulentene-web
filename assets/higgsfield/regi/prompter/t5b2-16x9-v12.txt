The first frame is the start frame itself, same framing, same scale. One continuous crane shot in one street; the view stays clear, nothing passes in front of the lens, and the view never dissolves or blends. 0-1.5s: the courier in the yellow cap pushes off and rides his black bicycle slowly to the left along the street, past the bakery door; the camera stays where it is. 1.5-6s: one slow, smooth crane up and back, rising steadily high above the street while he rides on to the left past the woman at the window and along the tram rails, until the wide view of the whole street at dusk matches the end frame. The woman stands still at the bakery window, the plumber stands still at the van and the tram stands still far back the whole time; nobody enters or leaves the frame. 6-8s: the camera stops and holds completely still for the last two seconds; the courier is small in the street at the lower left, in profile on his bicycle, exactly as in the end frame. Figures are seen from behind or in profile, never facing the camera. Clay miniature, steady amber light, exactly one moon, smooth continuous motion, everything stays solid and opaque, blank unmarked surfaces.
