The first frame is the start frame itself: same framing, and the courier is seen from behind on his bicycle exactly where he is in the start frame. One continuous crane shot. 0-2s: the camera is locked off; the courier, seen from behind, rides his bicycle straight away from the camera along the street and the tram rolls slowly far back. 2-4s: the tram stops and the courier freezes mid-pedal, upright on the motionless bicycle; the camera stays locked off and nothing moves for two full seconds. 4-9s: crane up and pull back; the wooden baseboard edge of the model slides into view, then a real workbench; a blank yellow paper note, a pencil and an empty mug pass large and soft along the bottom of the frame, the view above them stays clear. 9-13s: keep pulling back, rack focus from the tiny street to the room; the two monitors already show the lime-green check shapes when they enter the frame, the screens never switch on; an empty chair. 13-15s: the camera holds completely still on the end frame for the last two seconds. The figures stay frozen, still clear air, steady light, blank unmarked surfaces.
