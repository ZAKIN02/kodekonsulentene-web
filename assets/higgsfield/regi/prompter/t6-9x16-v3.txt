One continuous vertical crane shot. 0-1s: the tram rolls and the figures walk. 1-2s: the tram stops and every figure freezes mid-step. 2-7s: crane straight up and tilt back; the wooden baseboard edge of the model slides in from the bottom, then a real workbench; a blank yellow paper note, a pencil and an empty mug pass huge and soft close to the lens. 7-13s: keep rising, rack focus from the tiny street to the room above it; the warm disc becomes the shade of a desk lamp; the two monitors already show the lime-green check shapes when they enter the frame, the screens never switch on; an empty chair. 13-15s: the camera stops and holds still on the end frame for the last two seconds. Still clear air, steady light, the figures stay frozen, all surfaces blank and unmarked.
