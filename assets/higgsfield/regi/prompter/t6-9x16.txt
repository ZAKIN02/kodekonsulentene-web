One continuous vertical crane shot. 0-2s: hold; the blue tram glides to a halt and every clay figure freezes, the courier in the yellow cap stopped mid-step. 2-7s: crane straight up and tilt back; the wooden baseboard edge of the model slides in from the bottom, then a real workbench; a blank yellow paper note, a pencil and a coffee mug pass huge and soft close to the lens. 7-12s: keep rising, rack focus from the tiny street to the room above it: the warm disc in the sky becomes a desk lamp, then two monitors with lime-green check shapes and an empty chair fill the upper half. 12-15s: settle completely still on the end frame. The street is now a small handmade model on a photoreal desk. Warm amber and deep blue, tack sharp, all surfaces blank and unmarked.
